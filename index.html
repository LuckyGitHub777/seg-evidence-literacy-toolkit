What does a sentence actually establish?

Practice labeling the language of a record before evaluating its truth. These constructed examples teach editorial distinctions; no laboratory result, measurement, device, mechanism or extraordinary claim is being verified.

CASE 01 / 04
A fictional lab notebook says an instrument displayed 3.2 V at 10:15.
Ready for classificationThe goal is careful description, not an invented proof.

The most suitable label depends on how the sentence is framed. Distinguish what someone reported, what might explain it, and what evidence would be needed to validate either. For real studies, consult qualified researchers and safety authorities.

Documentation is not replication.

A blank trial log makes recordkeeping easier; it does not demonstrate a physical effect or establish causation.

Safety is independent of curiosity.

A proposed experiment still requires qualified safety assessment, proper facilities and authorization. None are granted by this toolkit.
