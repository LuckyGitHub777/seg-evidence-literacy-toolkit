These are vocabulary distinctions for source review. Choosing a category does not prove that a particular claim fits it.

01

Recorded observation

A traceable record of what an observer or instrument reported. A record can be genuine while its interpretation is still wrong.

Evidence needed

Source provenance, date, instrument/context details, controls, uncertainty and independent review.

Documentation is not replication.

A blank trial log makes recordkeeping easier; it does not demonstrate a physical effect or establish causation.

Safety is independent of curiosity.

A proposed experiment still requires qualified safety assessment, proper facilities and authorization. None are granted by this toolkit.
